# 📕 Nepali

## Overview
This book covers the Nepali language, including vocabulary, grammar, and writing skills.

---

## 🧠 Important Flash Cards

**Q1. What does Nepali language study include?**  
**A:** It includes vocabulary, grammar, reading, and writing.

**Q2. Why is vocabulary important?**  
**A:** It helps us understand and express ideas.

**Q3. Why should we practise Nepali writing?**  
**A:** Practice helps us write clearly and correctly.

**Q4. What is a शब्द (शब्द)?**  
**A:** A शब्द is a meaningful word used in language.

**Q5. What is a वाक्य?**  
**A:** वाक्य is a group of words that gives complete meaning.

**Q6. What is a कथा?**  
**A:** कथा is a story that tells a series of events.

**Q7. What is a कविता?**  
**A:** कविता is a poem written with ideas, feelings, and rhythm.

**Q8. Why is reading Nepali important?**  
**A:** It improves our language, knowledge, and understanding.

**Q9. What is लेखन?**  
**A:** लेखन means expressing ideas in written form.

**Q10. Why should we learn new Nepali words?**  
**A:** New words help us speak and write better.
