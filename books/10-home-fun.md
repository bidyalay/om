# 🏠 Home Fun

## Overview
This book contains fun activities and learning materials for home enjoyment.

---

## 🧠 Important Flash Cards

**Q1. Why are home activities useful?**  
**A:** They can make learning fun and help us practise skills.

**Q2. What can we learn at home?**  
**A:** We can learn through reading, activities, observation, and practice.

**Q3. Why should we help at home?**  
**A:** Helping at home teaches responsibility and useful life skills.

**Q4. What is a household chore?**  
**A:** A household chore is a useful task done to help take care of the home.

**Q5. Why should we keep our room tidy?**  
**A:** A tidy room is easier to use and helps us find things.

**Q6. Why is teamwork important at home?**  
**A:** Teamwork helps family members complete tasks together.

**Q7. What is creativity?**  
**A:** Creativity is the ability to think of new ideas and make useful or interesting things.

**Q8. Why are hobbies useful?**  
**A:** Hobbies can help us learn skills and use our free time well.

**Q9. Why is reading at home helpful?**  
**A:** Reading improves knowledge, vocabulary, and imagination.

**Q10. Why should we organize our study time?**  
**A:** Good organization helps us finish work and make time for rest and fun.

### 🏡 Fun Learning Activities
- Read a short book and tell someone what you learned.
- Draw a picture of your home.
- Help organize your study table.
- Make a simple list of daily tasks.
- Observe plants or animals around your home and write what you notice.

### ✍️ Practice
- Write five things you can do to help at home.
- Write about your favourite hobby.
- Make a simple daily study timetable.
