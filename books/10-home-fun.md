# 🏠 Home Fun

## Overview
This book contains fun activities and learning materials for home enjoyment.

---

## 🧠 Important Flash Cards

**Q1. Why are home activities useful?**  
**A:** They can make learning fun and help us practise skills.

**Q2. What can we learn at home?**  
**A:** We can learn through reading, activities, observation, and practice.

**Q3. Why should we help at home?**  
**A:** Helping at home teaches responsibility and useful life skills.
