# 🔬 Science

## Overview
This book covers scientific concepts, experiments, and natural world phenomena.

---

## 🧠 Important Flash Cards

**Q1. What is science?**  
**A:** Science is the study of the natural world.

**Q2. What is an experiment?**  
**A:** An experiment is a test done to learn or check something.

**Q3. What are natural phenomena?**  
**A:** They are events that happen in nature, such as rain, wind, and earthquakes.

**Q4. Why do we study science?**  
**A:** Science helps us understand nature and how things work.
