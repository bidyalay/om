# 🔬 Science

## Overview
This book covers scientific concepts, experiments, and natural world phenomena.

---

## 🧠 Important Flash Cards

**Q1. What is science?**  
**A:** Science is the study of the natural world.

**Q2. What is an experiment?**  
**A:** An experiment is a test done to learn or check something.

**Q3. What are natural phenomena?**  
**A:** They are events that happen in nature, such as rain, wind, and earthquakes.

**Q4. Why do we study science?**  
**A:** Science helps us understand nature and how things work.

**Q5. What are living things?**  
**A:** Living things grow, need food or energy, reproduce, and carry out life processes.

**Q6. What are non-living things?**  
**A:** Non-living things do not carry out life processes like living things do.

**Q7. What is photosynthesis?**  
**A:** Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants make food using sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide.

**Q8. Why do plants need sunlight?**  
**A:** Green plants need sunlight to make food during photosynthesis.

**Q9. What are the three common states of matter?**  
**A:** Solid, liquid, and gas.

**Q10. What is evaporation?**  
**A:** Evaporation is the change of a liquid into a gas.

**Q11. What is condensation?**  
**A:** Condensation is the change of a gas into a liquid.

**Q12. What is force?**  
**A:** Force is a push or pull that can change the motion or shape of an object.

**Q13. What is sound?**  
**A:** Sound is produced by vibrations and travels through a medium.

**Q14. What is light?**  
**A:** Light is a form of energy that allows us to see objects.

**Q15. What is the water cycle?**  
**A:** The water cycle is the continuous movement of water through evaporation, condensation, and precipitation.

**Q16. What is a habitat?**  
**A:** A habitat is the natural home of a plant or animal.

**Q17. Why should we protect the environment?**  
**A:** A healthy environment is important for people, animals, and plants.

### 🔬 Simple Science Activities
- Observe how a shadow changes during the day.
- Observe a plant and record its growth.
- Put ice in a container and observe melting.
- Record the weather for one week.

### ✍️ Practice
- Draw the three states of matter.
- Draw and label a simple water cycle.
- Write five uses of plants.
- Write three ways to protect nature.
