## 🧠 Important Flash Cards

**Q1. What is geography?**  
**A:** Geography is the study of places, land, people, and the Earth.

**Q2. What is history?**  
**A:** History is the study of past events.

**Q3. What is culture?**  
**A:** Culture is the way of life, traditions, and customs of people.

**Q4. Why do we study social studies?**  
**A:** It helps us understand people, places, society, and our past.
