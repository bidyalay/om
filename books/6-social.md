## 🧠 Important Flash Cards

**Q1. What is geography?**  
**A:** Geography is the study of places, land, people, and the Earth.

**Q2. What is history?**  
**A:** History is the study of past events.

**Q3. What is culture?**  
**A:** Culture is the way of life, traditions, and customs of people.

**Q4. Why do we study social studies?**  
**A:** It helps us understand people, places, society, and our past.

**Q5. What is a community?**  
**A:** A community is a group of people living or working together in an area.

**Q6. What is a map?**  
**A:** A map is a drawing that shows places on the Earth's surface.

**Q7. What is a natural resource?**  
**A:** A natural resource is something from nature that people use, such as water, soil, and forests.

**Q8. What are rights?**  
**A:** Rights are basic freedoms and protections people should have.

**Q9. What are duties?**  
**A:** Duties are responsibilities we should perform.

**Q10. Why should we respect different cultures?**  
**A:** Respect helps people live together peacefully.

**Q11. What is the environment?**  
**A:** The environment includes the living and non-living things around us.

**Q12. Why should we protect heritage?**  
**A:** Heritage connects us with our history and culture.

**Q13. What is a local government?**  
**A:** It is a government that manages services and development in a local area.
