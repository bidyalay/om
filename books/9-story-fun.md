## 🧠 Important Flash Cards

**Q1. What is a story?**  
**A:** A story is a series of events about characters.

**Q2. Who are characters?**  
**A:** Characters are the people, animals, or other beings in a story.

**Q3. What is the lesson of a story?**  
**A:** It is the important idea or moral we learn from the story.

**Q4. Why are stories useful?**  
**A:** Stories entertain us and can teach us good lessons.

**Q5. What is a setting?**  
**A:** The setting tells where and when a story happens.

**Q6. What is a plot?**  
**A:** The plot is the series of events that happen in a story.

**Q7. What is a narrator?**  
**A:** The narrator is the voice that tells the story.

**Q8. What is a moral?**  
**A:** A moral is the lesson that a story teaches us.

**Q9. What is a dialogue?**  
**A:** Dialogue is a conversation between characters.

**Q10. Why should we retell a story?**  
**A:** Retelling helps us remember the important events and understand the story.

### 📚 Story Reading Tips
1. Read the title first.
2. Notice the main characters.
3. Find the setting.
4. Look for the important events.
5. Think about the lesson.

### ✍️ Practice
- Write the names of three characters from a story you know.
- Write the setting of your favourite story.
- Write one lesson you learned from a story.
