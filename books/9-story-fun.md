## 🧠 Important Flash Cards

**Q1. What is a story?**  
**A:** A story is a series of events about characters.

**Q2. Who are characters?**  
**A:** Characters are the people, animals, or other beings in a story.

**Q3. What is the lesson of a story?**  
**A:** It is the important idea or moral we learn from the story.

**Q4. Why are stories useful?**  
**A:** Stories entertain us and can teach us good lessons.
