# ❤️ Health

## Overview
This book covers health, wellness, nutrition, and fitness topics.

---

## 🧠 Important Flash Cards

**Q1. What is good health?**  
**A:** Good health means keeping our body and mind healthy.

**Q2. Why is nutrition important?**  
**A:** Good nutrition gives our body energy and helps us grow.

**Q3. Why should we exercise?**  
**A:** Exercise helps keep our body strong and active.

**Q4. What is wellness?**  
**A:** Wellness means taking good care of our physical and mental well-being.
