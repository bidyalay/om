# ❤️ Health

## Overview
This book covers health, wellness, nutrition, and fitness topics.

---

## 🧠 Important Flash Cards

**Q1. What is good health?**  
**A:** Good health means keeping our body and mind healthy.

**Q2. Why is nutrition important?**  
**A:** Good nutrition gives our body energy and helps us grow.

**Q3. Why should we exercise?**  
**A:** Exercise helps keep our body strong and active.

**Q4. What is wellness?**  
**A:** Wellness means taking good care of our physical and mental well-being.

**Q5. Why should we wash our hands?**  
**A:** Handwashing helps remove germs and prevent illness.

**Q6. What is a balanced diet?**  
**A:** A balanced diet contains different kinds of nutritious foods in suitable amounts.

**Q7. Why do we need water?**  
**A:** Water helps our body work properly and prevents dehydration.

**Q8. Why is sleep important?**  
**A:** Sleep gives our body and mind time to rest and recover.

**Q9. What is personal hygiene?**  
**A:** Personal hygiene means keeping our body clean and healthy.

**Q10. Why should we keep our surroundings clean?**  
**A:** Clean surroundings help reduce germs, waste, and disease.

**Q11. What is first aid?**  
**A:** First aid is basic help given to someone who is injured or suddenly unwell until proper medical help is available.

**Q12. Why should we care for our mental well-being?**  
**A:** It helps us handle feelings, learn, and enjoy daily life.

### 🥗 Healthy Habits
- Eat a variety of nutritious foods.
- Drink enough clean water.
- Stay active every day.
- Keep yourself and your surroundings clean.
- Get enough sleep.
