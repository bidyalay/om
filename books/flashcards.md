# 📚 Class 5 Important Flash Cards

Simple question-and-answer cards for quick revision of all 11 books.

> **Note:** These are simple general revision flashcards based on the topics described in the books folder.

---

## 1. 📘 English

**Q1. What is vocabulary?**  
**A:** Vocabulary is the collection of words we know and use.

**Q2. What is reading comprehension?**  
**A:** It means understanding what we read.

**Q3. Why is writing important?**  
**A:** Writing helps us share our ideas clearly.

---

## 2. 📗 Grammar

**Q1. What is a sentence?**  
**A:** A sentence is a group of words that gives a complete meaning.

**Q2. What is a noun?**  
**A:** A noun is a naming word for a person, place, animal, or thing.

**Q3. What is a verb?**  
**A:** A verb is an action or doing word.

**Q4. Why do we learn grammar?**  
**A:** Grammar helps us use language correctly.

---

## 3. 📕 Nepali

**Q1. What does Nepali language study include?**  
**A:** It includes vocabulary, grammar, reading, and writing.

**Q2. Why is vocabulary important?**  
**A:** It helps us understand and express ideas.

**Q3. Why should we practise Nepali writing?**  
**A:** Practice helps us write clearly and correctly.

---

## 4. 📖 Vyakaran

**Q1. What is Vyakaran?**  
**A:** Vyakaran means grammar. It gives rules for using language correctly.

**Q2. What does Nepali Vyakaran teach?**  
**A:** It teaches language rules and sentence structures.

**Q3. Why is correct sentence structure important?**  
**A:** It makes our meaning clear.

---

## 5. 📐 Maths

**Q1. What is addition?**  
**A:** Addition means putting numbers together to find the total.

**Q2. What is subtraction?**  
**A:** Subtraction means taking one number away from another.

**Q3. What is multiplication?**  
**A:** Multiplication is repeated addition.

**Q4. What is division?**  
**A:** Division means sharing a number into equal parts.

**Q5. Why do we learn formulas?**  
**A:** Formulas help us solve mathematical problems correctly.

---

## 6. 🌍 Social

**Q1. What is geography?**  
**A:** Geography is the study of places, land, people, and the Earth.

**Q2. What is history?**  
**A:** History is the study of past events.

**Q3. What is culture?**  
**A:** Culture is the way of life, traditions, and customs of people.

**Q4. Why do we study social studies?**  
**A:** It helps us understand people, places, society, and our past.

---

## 7. ❤️ Health

**Q1. What is good health?**  
**A:** Good health means keeping our body and mind healthy.

**Q2. Why is nutrition important?**  
**A:** Good nutrition gives our body energy and helps us grow.

**Q3. Why should we exercise?**  
**A:** Exercise helps keep our body strong and active.

**Q4. What is wellness?**  
**A:** Wellness means taking good care of our physical and mental well-being.

---

## 8. 💻 Computer

**Q1. What is a computer?**  
**A:** A computer is an electronic machine that processes information.

**Q2. What is programming?**  
**A:** Programming means giving instructions to a computer.

**Q3. What is technology?**  
**A:** Technology is the use of knowledge and tools to solve problems and do work.

**Q4. What are digital skills?**  
**A:** Digital skills are skills used to work safely and effectively with digital devices and information.

---

## 9. 📖 Story Fun

**Q1. What is a story?**  
**A:** A story is a series of events about characters.

**Q2. Who are characters?**  
**A:** Characters are the people, animals, or other beings in a story.

**Q3. What is the lesson of a story?**  
**A:** It is the important idea or moral we learn from the story.

**Q4. Why are stories useful?**  
**A:** Stories entertain us and can teach us good lessons.

---

## 10. 🏠 Home Fun

**Q1. Why are home activities useful?**  
**A:** They can make learning fun and help us practise skills.

**Q2. What can we learn at home?**  
**A:** We can learn through reading, activities, observation, and practice.

**Q3. Why should we help at home?**  
**A:** Helping at home teaches responsibility and useful life skills.

---

## 11. 🔬 Science

**Q1. What is science?**  
**A:** Science is the study of the natural world.

**Q2. What is an experiment?**  
**A:** An experiment is a test done to learn or check something.

**Q3. What are natural phenomena?**  
**A:** They are events that happen in nature, such as rain, wind, and earthquakes.

**Q4. Why do we study science?**  
**A:** Science helps us understand nature and how things work.

## ⭐ Quick Revision Tip
Read the **question first**, try to answer it yourself, and then check the **answer**. This is a good way to remember important points.
