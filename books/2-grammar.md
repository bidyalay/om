# 📗 Grammar

## Overview
This book focuses on English grammar rules, sentence structure, and proper language usage.

---

## 🧠 Important Flash Cards

**Q1. What is a sentence?**  
**A:** A sentence is a group of words that gives a complete meaning.

**Q2. What is a noun?**  
**A:** A noun is a naming word for a person, place, animal, or thing.

**Q3. What is a verb?**  
**A:** A verb is an action or doing word.

**Q4. Why do we learn grammar?**  
**A:** Grammar helps us use language correctly.

**Q5. What is a pronoun?**  
**A:** A pronoun is a word used instead of a noun. Example: he, she, it, they.

**Q6. What is an adjective?**  
**A:** An adjective describes a noun. Example: a beautiful flower.

**Q7. What is an adverb?**  
**A:** An adverb tells us more about a verb. Example: runs quickly.

**Q8. What is a preposition?**  
**A:** A preposition shows the relation between words. Example: in, on, under.

**Q9. What is a conjunction?**  
**A:** A conjunction joins words or sentences. Example: and, but, because.

**Q10. What are articles?**  
**A:** The articles are a, an, and the.

**Q11. What is a singular noun?**  
**A:** It names one person, place, animal, or thing.

**Q12. What is a plural noun?**  
**A:** It names more than one. Example: book — books.

**Q13. What is punctuation?**  
**A:** Punctuation marks help make writing clear. Examples: . , ? !
