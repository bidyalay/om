# 📗 Grammar

## Overview
This book focuses on English grammar rules, sentence structure, and proper language usage.

---

## 🧠 Important Flash Cards

**Q1. What is a sentence?**  
**A:** A sentence is a group of words that gives a complete meaning.

**Q2. What is a noun?**  
**A:** A noun is a naming word for a person, place, animal, or thing.

**Q3. What is a verb?**  
**A:** A verb is an action or doing word.

**Q4. Why do we learn grammar?**  
**A:** Grammar helps us use language correctly.
