# 📐 Maths

## Overview
This book covers mathematical concepts, formulas, problem-solving techniques, and numerical operations.

---

## 🧠 Important Flash Cards

**Q1. What is addition?**  
**A:** Addition means putting numbers together to find the total.

**Q2. What is subtraction?**  
**A:** Subtraction means taking one number away from another.

**Q3. What is multiplication?**  
**A:** Multiplication is repeated addition.

**Q4. What is division?**  
**A:** Division means sharing a number into equal parts.

**Q5. Why do we learn formulas?**  
**A:** Formulas help us solve mathematical problems correctly.

**Q6. What are even numbers?**  
**A:** Numbers that can be divided by 2 without a remainder are even numbers.

**Q7. What are odd numbers?**  
**A:** Numbers that are not exactly divisible by 2 are odd numbers.

**Q8. What is a fraction?**  
**A:** A fraction represents a part of a whole.

**Q9. What is a decimal?**  
**A:** A decimal is a number written using a decimal point.

**Q10. What is a percentage?**  
**A:** A percentage means a part out of 100.

**Q11. What is perimeter?**  
**A:** Perimeter is the distance around a shape.

**Q12. What is area?**  
**A:** Area is the amount of surface inside a shape.

**Q13. What is HCF?**  
**A:** HCF is the greatest common factor of two or more numbers.

**Q14. What is LCM?**  
**A:** LCM is the smallest common multiple of two or more numbers.

**Q15. What is an angle?**  
**A:** An angle is formed when two lines or rays meet at a point.

### 🧮 Quick Formulas
- Rectangle perimeter = 2 × (length + breadth)
- Rectangle area = length × breadth
- Percentage = (part ÷ whole) × 100
