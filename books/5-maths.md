# 📐 Maths

## Overview
This book covers mathematical concepts, formulas, problem-solving techniques, and numerical operations.

---

## 🧠 Important Flash Cards

**Q1. What is addition?**  
**A:** Addition means putting numbers together to find the total.

**Q2. What is subtraction?**  
**A:** Subtraction means taking one number away from another.

**Q3. What is multiplication?**  
**A:** Multiplication is repeated addition.

**Q4. What is division?**  
**A:** Division means sharing a number into equal parts.

**Q5. Why do we learn formulas?**  
**A:** Formulas help us solve mathematical problems correctly.
