# 📖 Vyakaran

## Overview
This book covers Nepali grammar (Vyakaran) rules and language structures.

---

## 🧠 Important Flash Cards

**Q1. What is Vyakaran?**  
**A:** Vyakaran means grammar. It gives rules for using language correctly.

**Q2. What does Nepali Vyakaran teach?**  
**A:** It teaches language rules and sentence structures.

**Q3. Why is correct sentence structure important?**  
**A:** It makes our meaning clear.

**Q4. What is वर्ण?**  
**A:** वर्ण is the smallest unit of spoken language sound represented in writing.

**Q5. What are स्वर वर्ण?**  
**A:** स्वर वर्ण are vowel sounds that can be spoken independently.

**Q6. What are व्यञ्जन वर्ण?**  
**A:** व्यञ्जन वर्ण are consonant sounds that usually need a vowel sound.

**Q7. What is नाम?**  
**A:** नाम is a word used to name a person, place, animal, or thing.

**Q8. What is क्रिया?**  
**A:** क्रिया is a word that shows an action or state.

**Q9. What is समानार्थी शब्द?**  
**A:** समानार्थी शब्द are words with similar meanings.

**Q10. What is विपरीतार्थी शब्द?**  
**A:** विपरीतार्थी शब्द are words with opposite meanings.

**Q11. What is लिङ्ग?**  
**A:** लिङ्ग shows whether a word refers to masculine, feminine, or another grammatical category.

**Q12. What is वचन?**  
**A:** वचन shows whether something is one or more than one.

**Q13. What is punctuation?**  
**A:** Punctuation marks help make sentences clear and easy to read.
