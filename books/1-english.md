# 📘 English

## Overview
This book covers English language skills including vocabulary, reading comprehension, and writing techniques.

---

## 🧠 Important Flash Cards

**Q1. What is vocabulary?**  
**A:** Vocabulary is the collection of words we know and use.

**Q2. What is reading comprehension?**  
**A:** It means understanding what we read.

**Q3. Why is writing important?**  
**A:** Writing helps us share our ideas clearly.

**Q4. What is a paragraph?**  
**A:** A paragraph is a group of sentences about one main idea.

**Q5. What is a synonym?**  
**A:** A synonym is a word with a similar meaning. Example: big — large.

**Q6. What is an antonym?**  
**A:** An antonym is a word with the opposite meaning. Example: hot — cold.

**Q7. What is a sentence?**  
**A:** A sentence is a group of words that gives complete meaning.

**Q8. What are the main parts of a simple sentence?**  
**A:** A simple sentence usually has a subject and a predicate.

**Q9. What is a story?**  
**A:** A story is a set of events with characters and a plot.

**Q10. Why should we read books?**  
**A:** Reading improves our knowledge, vocabulary, and imagination.
